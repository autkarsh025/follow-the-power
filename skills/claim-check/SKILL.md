---
name: claim-check
description: Check a number or claim the user states (e.g. "my CFO said revenue was $X") against the data
---
1. Treat every number in the question as a CLAIM, not a fact.
2. Find the relevant figure with the right tool (company_financials, quarterly_financials, power_index, ...).
3. Compare with `calculate` (difference and % difference).
4. Answer plainly first: "That's not right" / "That matches". Then give the correct figure with its quarter.
5. If the claim can't be checked with the available data, say exactly that; never accept it by default.
6. You may repeat the claimed number when rebutting it, but never present it as a fact.
